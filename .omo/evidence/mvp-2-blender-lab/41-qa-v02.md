## Seam / gap / scale notes (honest findings)

- FLOOR (good surprise): the refined thumbnail (30-thumb-KIT_floor_1m.png) showed a
  see-through black/white lattice, but that was the THUMBNAIL's rendering of groove
  relief. The actual normalized mesh renders as a SOLID slab with a grey/white
  terrazzo-shard bake. No holes, no see-through. Palette fallback NOT needed;
  FLOOR_FORCE_FALLBACK left False. Tile seams visible at 1 m grid — reads as
  intentional tiling, slightly busier than guide terrazzo.
- FLOOR texture content: baked shards are angular cross-hatch remnants of the groove
  relief, not true terrazzo speckle. Acceptable at eye level; user judges at 2b.
- WALLS: Meshy wall_1m panels (cream + teal bottom strip, 4 corner bolts) tile cleanly;
  bolt heads repeat every 1 m — reads as paneling. Slight texture seam at panel edges
  where the teal strip meets, not distracting at eye level. In kit_compare the solid
  wall texture is subtly mottled vs flat bpy white — visible difference, by design.
- DOOR: stretched 0.554->1.0 x, 0.952->3.2 z (nonuniform). As a closed full-height
  teal 4-panel leaf it reads plausibly as a tall door; the 4 panels elongate but do
  not look broken. Chrome lever + red lock dots visible at correct hand height-ish
  (stretched up). Honest: proportions are nonstandard (3.2 m door leaf, no transom).
- Z-FIGHTING: none seen — tile tops at +0.005 clear slab_kit_floor top (z 0) and
  ROOM_meeting_a_kit marker is hide_render.
- DOUBLE WALLS: none — only one wall run per side in the kit room.
- SCALE vs furniture: chairs/table identical bpy builders in both rooms; kit pieces
  normalized to identical envelope => scale coherent.
- PILLAR: normalized but not placed in the room (matches bpy room which has no
  interior pillar); present in KIT_MESHY only, listed KIT_UNUSED_OK.
- Lighting parity: duplicate area light at +30, ceiling slab underside at z 3.0 both
  rooms; mean_lum 0.670 vs 0.693 (kit slightly brighter — brighter tile bake), close.
