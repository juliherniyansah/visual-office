## Design
- New FloorLoadBoundary.tsx wraps ONLY <Canvas>; FloorSelector is a sibling, outside every boundary a model-load error can trip.
- No extra DOM when healthy; no key on boundary -> Canvas NOT remounted on floor change (lazy-load/round-trip assertions still pass).
- isModelLoadError(/Could not load|\.glb|Failed to fetch|NetworkError|net::/i); other errors rethrown in render() -> outer WebGLErrorBoundary still shows 'WebGL not available'.
- Reset: componentDidUpdate when resetKey (currentFloor) changes, and 'Coba lagi' button. Both call useGLTF.clear(url) first, because drei caches the rejected promise and would rethrow it.
- Retry verified empirically: after page.unroute, clicking F3 fetched floor_3.glb (200), __hqStats.floorId==3, error text gone (assertion in the test).
- 'Coba lagi' button click not covered by an automated assertion (same reset path as floor change); screenshot shows it rendered.
